# Surface brief: SFT STELLAR console (all pages)

Scope: Console, Hardware, Firmware, Settings. Mode: Operate. Audience: Stellar Kinetics test-stand operators; bright hangar, dim control room and outdoor laptop; 35-inch main screen.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The console is a live launch broadcast of our own test: the stand camera is the stage and telemetry is overlaid on it, refusing the category default of identical dark cards in a grid.

OWN-WORLD: Pure black ground, hairline white rules (14-18% white) instead of boxes, white type in Barlow 300-400 with wide-tracked caps for small labels and Barlow Condensed 300 for big numerals. Colour only for state: safe green, armed amber, fire orange, stop red. Square edges, no shadows, no card chrome.

STORY: The operator sees the stand, the pressure and the state at once, follows the test as it moves along a timeline, aborts in one key, and ends with a saved result.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (brand, nav, system dots, clock). Left: full-bleed stage (camera, or pressure trace when no camera; switchable). Lower third over the stage: chamber-pressure arc gauge left, T-minus centre with the test timeline READY-ARMED-COUNTDOWN-IGNITION-SAFED, peak gauge right. Right rail 360px: ignition state word, key values, ARM and hold-to-start, red ABORT, last result, event log.

FORM: Launch webcast graphics (pick card, chosen by owner over assigned safety-placard direction). seed:097768eb740f8564

SIGNATURE: the test timeline under the countdown that advances with each state, plus arc gauges overlaid on the live picture.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
