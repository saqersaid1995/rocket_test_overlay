    Pressure

    Sensor calibration (zero voltage, slope) is set on the ESP32's own page: join the Wi-Fi “PressureMonitor”, then open http://192.168.4.1.

    Thrust

    No load cell? SFT STELLAR can estimate thrust from chamber pressure and the nozzle size: thrust = efficiency × Cf × chamber pressure × throat area.

    Enter the throat and exit diameters to see the numbers.

    γ values for sugar propellants are the two-phase values from R. Nakka (nakka-rocketry.net). Efficiency 0.90–0.95 covers real nozzle losses. This is an estimate, not a measurement: expect about ±5–10% when the dimensions are right.
